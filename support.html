Getting started

What do I need to use Huddle?

An iPhone signed in to iCloud, with iCloud Drive turned on. Check in Settings → [your name] → iCloud. Each member of your household needs their own Apple device and iCloud account.

How do I set up a household?
  1. Open Huddle and tap Create a household.
  2. Give it a name and choose your currency.
  3. Tap Continue. You're the household's creator.
How do I invite my partner?
  1. Go to Household and tap Invite member.
  2. Send the invite with Messages, Mail or a copied link.
  3. Your partner installs Huddle, opens the invite on their phone and taps Join household.
How do I add an expense or income?

Type the amount and what it's for in the bar at the bottom, for example 126.40 groceries, and tap send. Or tap + to choose income or expense, a category, a date, a note and a receipt photo.

Syncing and sharing

My partner's entries aren't showing up
  1. Check both phones are online and signed in to iCloud.
  2. Pull down on the activity feed to refresh.
  3. Make sure your partner opened the invite on the iCloud account it was sent to.
  4. If it still doesn't sync, close Huddle completely and open it again.
It says "iCloud account required"

Sign in to iCloud in Settings and turn on iCloud Drive, then open Huddle again.

The invite link does nothing

Install Huddle from the App Store first, then tap the invite link again.

My iCloud storage is full

The household is stored in the creator's iCloud, so new entries can't sync if their storage is full. Free up space or upgrade storage in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

Your household

How do I fix a mistake?

Tap the entry and choose Correct amount. Huddle records the correction so both of you can see what changed. You can change the category, note or date any time.

How do I leave a household or remove someone?

Go to Household → Members. Any member can leave; the creator can remove members.

How do I delete our data?

The household creator can delete the household in Household → Settings. This removes it from iCloud for everyone and can't be undone. Deleting the app alone doesn't delete the household.

Can my partner use Android or a computer?

Not yet. Huddle currently works on iPhone. Android and web are on our roadmap.

Does Huddle connect to my bank?

No. You add entries yourself, which keeps Huddle simple and private. Read our privacy policy for details.
