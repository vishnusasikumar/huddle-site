Getting started

What do I need to use Huddle?

An iPhone or iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later, signed in to iCloud. Check in Settings → [your name]. Each member of your household needs their own device and their own Apple Account.

How do I set up a household?
  1. Open Huddle and enter a Household name.
  2. Choose the household's Currency. It can't be changed later.
  3. Enter Your name and tap Create household. You're the household's owner.
How do I invite my partner?
  1. Go to the Household tab and tap Invite a member.
  2. Send the invite with Messages, Mail or Copy Link.
  3. Your partner installs Huddle, opens the link on their phone, enters their name and taps Continue.
How do I add an expense or income?

Type the amount and what it's for in the bar at the bottom of Activity, for example 126.40 groceries, and tap send. Start with + for income, like +3000 salary, and add @sam to record who paid. Or tap + next to the bar for the full form, with a date, category, note and who paid. More examples

Syncing and sharing

My partner's entries aren't showing up
  1. Check both phones are online and signed in to iCloud.
  2. Pull down on the activity feed to refresh, or tap Household → Sync now.
  3. Make sure your partner opened the invite on the iCloud account it was sent to.
  4. If it still doesn't sync, close Huddle completely and open it again.

An entry marked "Saved on this device. Waiting to sync." will send when you're back online.

It says "Can't connect to iCloud"

Sign in to iCloud in Settings, then tap Try again in Huddle.

The invite link does nothing

Install Huddle first (from TestFlight during the beta), then tap the invite link again.

My iCloud storage is full

The household is stored in the creator's iCloud, so new entries can't sync if their storage is full. Free up space or upgrade storage in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

Your household

How do I fix a mistake?

Tap the entry, change the amount and tap Save. Huddle saves it as a correction, and the entry's history keeps the original. You can change the category, note or date any time. To remove an entry, tap Delete entry; you can still see it under Filter → Show deleted entries.

An entry says "Needs review"

Two of you changed the same amount at the same time, or one of you was offline. Both changes count until one of you taps the entry and chooses Keep, or enters the right amount.

How do I leave a household or remove someone?

Any member can leave in Household → Leave household. Their entries stay, marked as from a former member. The owner can remove members from Household → Members.

How do I delete our data?

The household's owner can delete the household from the Household tab. This removes it from iCloud for everyone and can't be undone, so export a CSV first (Household → Export entries) if you want a copy. Deleting the app alone doesn't delete the household.

Can my partner use Android or a computer?

Not yet. Huddle works on iPhone and iPad, with Mac coming next. Android and web are on our roadmap.

Does Huddle connect to my bank?

No. You add entries yourself, which keeps Huddle simple and private. Read how Huddle protects your data and our privacy policy.

Join the beta

Huddle is in beta testing with households in New Zealand and beyond. To join as a pair, email us at vvssoftwaresolutionslimited@gmail.com and we'll send you a TestFlight invite.

Huddle works best tested in pairs: each of you on your own iPhone, signed in to iCloud with your own Apple Account. Download the beta guide (PDF) or watch the walkthrough video.

What we'd like you to try

  1. Add an entry on one phone with Huddle open on both. It should appear on the other within about 10 seconds.
  2. Turn on Airplane Mode, add three entries, then turn it off. All three should reach the other phone.
  3. Both change the same entry to different amounts, one phone offline, then reconnect. It should wait under Needs review until one of you taps Keep.
  4. Delete an entry, then find it under Filter → Show deleted entries.
  5. Add a monthly repeating entry in Upcoming that starts today. It should be added once, on both phones.
  6. Type entries your own way and tell us any line Huddle reads wrongly, exactly as typed.
  7. The member who joined taps Household → Leave household, then the owner invites them again.
  8. Delete Huddle and reinstall it. Your household should come back.
  9. Try App lock, and Household+ (purchases in the beta are free).
