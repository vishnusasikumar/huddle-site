Privacy policy

Effective 11 October 2026

The short version

  • Your household's data is stored in iCloud and shared only with the people you invite. We can't see it.
  • Huddle never asks for your bank login.
  • No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics. We don't sell or share your data.

Who we are

Huddle is made by VVS Software Solutions Ltd ("we", "us"), based in New Zealand. If you have a question about this policy or your data, email vvssoftwaresolutionslimited@gmail.com.

What Huddle stores

When you use Huddle, the app stores the information you enter:

  • your household's name and currency
  • the names of household members, as shown in their iCloud or chosen display name
  • income and expense entries: amounts, categories, dates, notes and who added or paid for them
  • categories and their monthly limits, repeating entries, and the history of corrections and deleted entries

Where it's stored

Huddle uses Apple's CloudKit service. Your household is stored in the iCloud account of the person who created it, and shared through Apple's iCloud sharing only with the members they invite. Each member's phone also keeps a copy so the app works offline.

Amounts, notes and names are stored as iCloud encrypted fields, which only household members' devices can read. Details such as dates and categories are regular iCloud fields so the app can sync and sort them. See exactly what's encrypted.

We don't run our own servers for Huddle, and we have no access to the data in your iCloud account. Apple provides iCloud storage under Apple's privacy policy. Your household's data counts towards the creator's iCloud storage.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for, store or access your bank or card logins.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We don't include third-party analytics or tracking tools.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your information with anyone.

Face ID, notifications and crash reports

Face ID or Touch ID lock is handled by iOS. Huddle only learns whether unlocking succeeded; your biometric data never leaves Apple's secure hardware.

Notifications, when Huddle adds them, will be created on your device from your shared household data, not sent from our servers.

Crash reports: if you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash and performance reports. These don't include your household's entries.

When you contact us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you, and delete support emails once they're no longer needed.

Your choices and control

  • Leave a household: any member can leave at any time. They lose access to its data.
  • Remove members: the household creator can remove members.
  • Export: download your household's entries as a CSV file in Household → Export entries.
  • Delete: the household creator can delete the household, which removes it from iCloud for everyone. Deleting the app alone doesn't delete the household from iCloud.
  • You can also manage Huddle's iCloud data in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Children

Huddle is designed for adults managing a household. It isn't directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Your rights

We follow the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 and the principles of the EU and UK GDPR and California's CCPA. Because your data stays in your own iCloud account, you can view, export and delete it directly in the app. If you have any other request, email us and we'll help.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Huddle handles data, for example by adding our own servers, we'll update this page and the effective date above, and tell you in the app before the change applies to you.
