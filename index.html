Why Huddle

Money you share, kept simple

Most budgeting apps ask you to connect your bank, learn a method, or both. Often one person ends up using the app and the other stops. Huddle sticks to what matters: either of you can record what happened, and you both see the same picture.

  • Adding money feels like sending a message. Type an amount and a category, and it's in.
  • You both see changes straight away. Your partner's phone updates within moments.
  • No bank login. You record what actually happened, so your bank details stay with your bank.
  • Neutral about spending. An entry is information, not a grade. Spending shows in plain ink, never red.

How it works

Set up in about five minutes

  1. One of you creates the householdGive it a name, choose the currency and add your name.
  2. Invite your partnerSend a link with Messages, Mail or any app. It uses Apple's iCloud sharing, so only people you invite can join.
  3. Add entries as they happenType them in like a text message. They show up on both phones, and the month's totals update.

Nothing quietly overwritten

A shared history you can both trust

When an amount changes, Huddle saves a correction rather than overwriting it. Each entry keeps its original amount and every change, and a deleted entry leaves the totals but stays in the history. If you both change the same entry at once, it waits under Needs review until one of you checks it.

Privacy

Private by design

Huddle has no servers of its own. Your household lives in the iCloud account of the person who created it, shared only with the people they invite. Amounts, notes and names are stored as iCloud encrypted fields. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
